Joby

A placement application tool built around one reusable profile

Joby helps university students apply for placements without rebuilding their CV and answers from scratch every time, while keeping them in control of what gets reused and what changes.

Application admin becomes a major time sink in second year

Applying for placements becomes a second workload alongside university. Repeated CV tailoring, near-identical application forms, re-entering the same information into different portals, and tracking submissions across companies add up to a massive amount of time and cognitive bandwidth, far more than it first appears.

The deeper cost isn't just time spent applying. A substantial portion of second year gets diverted away from coursework, technical projects, interview preparation, and learning new skills.

The student changes less than the application does

A student's underlying experience is largely the same across applications. What changes is the role, the company, the job description, and which experiences matter most and how they should be presented. Joby treats each placement as a new translation of the same underlying professional information, not a new application built from nothing.

Give Joby the facts once

Instead of repeatedly rebuilding their professional information, the student gives Joby their existing CV once. Joby organises that material into a reusable profile of experience, education, projects, skills and achievements. AI may help extract and structure it, but never silently decides that an interpretation is true.

Tailoring one application should not break the next one

Joby keeps three kinds of information separate: what is actually true about the student, how they usually present themselves, and what changes temporarily for one application. If these were stored as the same thing, every application edit could accidentally change the student's permanent profile or another CV version.
