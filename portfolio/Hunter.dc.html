Technical architecture

Hunter combines a Next.js / TypeScript frontend, FastAPI intelligence service, and Supabase/Postgres data layer.

The prediction layer uses Prophet for price forecasting, Isolation Forest for anomaly detection, and category-specific heuristics to turn price history into BUY / WAIT / BUY SOON signals.

Marketplace search is abstracted behind adapters for sources such as eBay, Vinted, and Skyscanner, allowing different provider APIs and scraping flows to feed a common listing model.

Product principles

{{ p.title }} {{ p.body }}

What if shopping stopped being something you repeatedly searched?

Instead of repeatedly opening marketplaces, setting filters and checking prices, the user could give an AI their preferences, constraints, budget, urgency and willingness to wait, and the agent could continuously watch the market, surfacing an opportunity only when conditions become favourable. This is direction, not a shipped capability.

hunter.ai turns shopping from a search problem into a decision problem.

By combining conversational intent, predictive pricing and explainable recommendation logic, the product explores what commerce could look like when AI helps determine not only what to buy, but when and why.

Ask less. Decide better.
