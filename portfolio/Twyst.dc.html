01 / HUMAN TOUCH

Keep the hospitality. Remove the friction.

Twyst handles the repetitive ordering, payment and status interactions, so staff can stay focused on the parts of service that actually benefit from a human touch.

02 / SELF-SERVICE, WITHOUT THE SELF-SERVICE FEEL

Guests stay in control without hospitality becoming impersonal.

Twyst lets guests handle browsing, ordering and payment themselves, while staff remain present for the moments where human service actually matters.

03 / SYNCHRONISED STATE

Customer and staff always see the same stage of the order.

As the order moves from paid to preparing, ready and served, both sides update together, keeping everyone aligned without needing to ask for status.

Outcome

Tap, order, pay.
